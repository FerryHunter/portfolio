About

01 · What I actually do

Seven years turning rough ideas into products that ship, hold up in production, and earn a second visit.

Process

Three phases, one loop

Understand the problem before drawing anything.

I start with the business and the people inside it: what the product is for, where users quietly give up, and what the team can realistically build this quarter. Interviews, funnel data, competitor teardowns, whatever it takes to stop designing on guesses.

  • User research
  • Competitive audit
  • User flows
  • Scope mapping

Make the obvious version, then make it better.

Wireframes before polish, always. I work in systems rather than screens (components, tokens, empty and error states), so the twentieth screen costs a fraction of the first and nothing falls apart at handoff.

  • Wireframing
  • UI design
  • Design systems
  • Prototyping

Design isn't finished until it's live.

I stay with engineers through build, spec the edge cases nobody drew, and review what shipped against what we designed. Then I watch how it performs and fix the parts that were wrong, because some of them always are.

  • Dev handoff
  • Design QA
  • Usability testing
  • Iteration
